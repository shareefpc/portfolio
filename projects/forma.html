Design concept / Product design

Forma

A mobile booking flow that helps people choose an appointment time and review it before they confirm.

The flow

Show available days first and reveal time slots once a person chooses a day. Keep each choice visible so they can change the time without starting over.

Interaction details

Pair the chosen date and time in the review step. Use clear labels, keyboard-friendly controls, and a confirmation message that repeats the booking details.

Try the booking flow

Choose a day and time from sample availability. The prototype simulates a booking.
